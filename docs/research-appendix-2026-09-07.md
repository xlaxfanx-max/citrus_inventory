### 2. California / Ventura County lemon market, 2025–2026

**Prices and grower returns.** June 2025 (Ag Alert via Sierra Sun Times): 70% fresh utilization at $18.50 FOB/carton; growing cost ~$6,500/acre; Ventura yields up to 1,050 cartons/acre; losses of $1,500–2,000/acre; at least three consecutive years of negative returns for many coastal growers; orchard removals and conversions to avocados; Canada's 25% retaliatory tariff on US lemons; Central Valley overproduction and better quality undercutting weather-damaged coastal fruit. Production costs up ~50% since 2020 ([FreshFruitPortal snippet, May 27, 2025](https://www.freshfruitportal.com/news/2025/05/27/california-citrus-growers-battle-production-cost-increases-in-a-depreciating-market/)). Terrain (Sept 23, 2025): Ventura lemon growers had "a particularly tough year in terms of price given the higher quality coming out of the Central Valley," while lemon acreage still grew ([Terrain](https://www.terrainag.com/insights/california-citrus-farmers-squeezed-in-2025/)).

USDA ERS (Mar 26, 2026): fresh lemon on-tree grower price Aug 2025–Jan 2026 averaged **$29.77/box** vs $30.43 prior year; 2025/26 US crop 1.08M tons (−3%), California 94% of output; exports +15% YTD (Canada 47%, Japan 26%, Korea 11%) ([ERS FTS-384 PDF](https://www.ers.usda.gov/media/20866/fts-384.pdf?v=55721)). NASS January 2026: California 25.0M boxes ([Citrus Industry](https://citrusindustry.net/2026/01/12/first-u-s-citrus-crop-forecast-issued-2025-26/)); July 2026 final forecast 26M boxes ([Citrus Industry](https://citrusindustry.net/2026/07/10/final-citrus-forecast-of-the-season-ends-on-a-positive-note/)).

**2026 in-season swing.** May 21, 2026: D1 nearly done, D2 ~40% harvested, D1 utilization ~80%, early color/green-fruit challenges resolving ([CCM Week 21](https://www.cacitrusmutual.com/2026/05/21/week-21-may-21-2026/)). June 2026: Limoneira reported pricing above $20/carton with forecasts of +$1/carton monthly through October. July 8, 2026: "extremely limited supplies and strong demand"; Argentina/Chile fruit through early December at 95–140 sizes, Mexico through late November at 165–235 ([Blue Book](https://www.bluebookservices.com/lemon-supplies-tighten-but-lime-sizing-improves/)). Aug 26, 2026: deficit to oversupply "in just a few weeks."

**Imports/tariffs.** Global supply down 6.6% to ~10.1M tons in 2025/26 on Türkiye's collapse; Mexico 3.37M tons (+3%), South Africa 840k tons (+3.7%) ([Citrus Industry, June 17, 2026](https://citrusindustry.net/2026/06/17/drop-global-lemon-supply/)). Argentine imports "significantly impacted District 2 grower returns" (CCM Aug 27, 2026).

**Ventura County.** 2025 lemon value $181,692,000, +54%; total ag $2.41B. Prior baseline: 14,407 lemon acres in 2019 ([UC ANR](https://ucanr.edu/blog/food-blog/article/uc-anr-study-outlines-costs-and-returns-producing-lemons-southern-california)). **Products (juice) price per ton was not found in any public 2025–26 source.**

### 4. Operational pain points reported by California lemon packers

- Fresh utilization and price below cost (Farm Bureau, above). D1 utilization ~80% in May 2026 (CCM).
- Color/green fruit: CCM's May 2026 bulletin notes early-season "challenges with green fruit."
- Storage decay: UC Riverside documented "high levels of sour rot on propiconazole-treated lemon fruit that was stored for extended times" in California packinghouses in 2020–21, with propiconazole resistance in *Geotrichum citri-aurantii* ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39441531/)). Many California houses "practice long in-house storage" ([Smilanick, UC](https://irrec.ifas.ufl.edu/postharvest/pdfs/past_events/2009_presentations/Smilanick_2009.pdf)).
- Import-timing whiplash: Aug 2026 oversupply "negatively impacted the spot market while inventories ballooned."
- Quality issues named in trade press: oil spotting and stylar-end problems (Blue Book snippet).

### 5. Sunkist initiatives (2025–2026)

- Sunkist i3 (IFPA Oct 2024): "investing in technology, innovating with process, implementing with people."
- Sienz minority investment (Mar 13, 2025); Sienz took over parts/service for Sunkist packinghouse-equipment customers.
- Sunkist + Fruit Growers Supply reorganization (effective Nov 1, 2025) under a new parent cooperative ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/sunkist-growers-inc-and-fruit-growers-supply-company-jointly-announce-strategic-reorganization-to-enhance-service-and-strengthen-market-position-302573205.html)).
- Limoneira rejoined Sunkist (Nov 1, 2025) ([Limoneira](https://investor.limoneira.com/news-releases/news-release-details/limoneira-announces-strategic-citrus-sales-and-marketing)).
- Whole-fruit / waste reduction with Ventura Coastal ([FreshPlaza](https://www.freshplaza.com/north-america/article/2137788/sunkist-case-study-from-grove-to-glass-and-beyond/)).
- No grower-facing digital tool or quality-data program was found.

### Implications (agent 1)

- Lead with the plant manager's own mandate: storage managed "to meet company standards for quality, size, and color."
- Frame for the CFO as process modernization and auditability.
- Position under Sunkist i3 vocabulary.
- ROI: use $18.50–$23/carton fresh FOB as the 2025–26 range; products price is not public. At ~3.5M products cartons/yr, moving 1% of products fruit to fresh at a ~$15/carton gap is ~$500k/yr (inference; validate on site).
- Packing cost falls sharply with throughput; a pack plan that smooths weekly volume has value independent of color forecasting.
- Features: market-regime flag; decay/hold-risk field alongside color; size-band awareness against the import calendar; explicit season-start calibration weeks.
- Risks: Saticoy's sorter/QC systems unconfirmed; Limoneira Q3 results Sept 9; manually read The Packer and FreshPlaza 2026 lemon coverage.

### Q5. Smartphone citrus color grading

Bao et al. 2023: image + N days → predicted rind image; CCI error 0.841 ([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10246884/)).
