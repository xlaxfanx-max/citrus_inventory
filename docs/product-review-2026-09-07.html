2. What the outside research says

Three agents, about 150 pages read, every claim sourced in the appendix. Inferences are marked as such.

Saticoy and the market

Saticoy is signalling modernisation. CFO Laura Troost was profiled in April 2026 as leading "modernizing legacy processes" and integrating accounting, operations and IT. CEO Marty Coert came up through operations. Saticoy's own plant-manager posting defines the job as managing storage "to meet company standards for quality, size, and color".

The market flipped in August 2026. June pricing passed $20 a carton for the first time since 2018, then Argentina and Chile shipped roughly double the planned volume and the spot market went to oversupply within weeks. Argentine imports are about twelve times their 2018 level and the 10 % tariff stayed. The pilot goes live in that environment.

Limoneira is the public proxy. Packing cost per carton swings from about $9 in high-volume quarters to $13 to $14.50 in low-volume quarters, so smoothing weekly volume has value on its own. Limoneira rejoined Sunkist in November 2025 and reports on 9 September. No public source gives a juice-lemon price; it has to come from Famous.

Decay is documented. UC Riverside found high sour rot on propiconazole-treated lemons stored for extended periods in California houses, with fungicide resistance.

Competitors and adjacent products

Nobody forecasts citrus colour over time. FreshCloud Storage and Strella are ethylene products for apples and pears. OneThird is near-infrared and skips citrus. Clarifresh, the incumbent QC app, has no shelf-life or ripeness prediction anywhere on its integration page.

Clarifresh is the capture tool to learn from: barcode scan that fills lot metadata from the ERP, several photos per defect, mandatory-field validation, six languages including Spanish, results pushed back to the ERP. Its adoption lesson: mirror the paper form the crew already uses.

Famous has no public API. It sells EDI, a QC tablet app, and Famous BI with scheduled delivery and conditional alerts. CSV plus scheduled BI exports is the realistic path, and the Monday email has to add forecasts to be read.

Phone colour science moved in 2026. A card with an 8×8 palette, four ArUco corners and histogram matching took class accuracy from 56 % to 100 %, but a glossy card fell to 19 to 25 %. White-balance mode, device and lighting interact.

Pilot, funding, workforce, science

Charge for the pilot. Paid pilots convert at 40 to 60 % against under 10 % for free trials. A 2026 specialty-crop example charges $10k and refunds if the grower cannot find twice that in savings. In a co-op, software is a house cost inside the per-carton packing charge: $20k a plant on a million cartons is about two cents a carton.

Funding calendar. CDFA Specialty Crop Block Grant 2027 concepts close 21 September 2026 and need a UC or nonprofit applicant. Citrus Research Board pre-proposals run in March; its post-harvest category wants "a clear path to commercialization". UC Lindcove has a packline with a Compac grader and a fruit-quality lab bookable by form.

Science that changes the model. Degreening is bell-shaped in temperature: fastest near 15 °C, slower at 5 °C, halted at 25 °C, and independent of ethylene. Storage life is 4 to 6 months at 10 °C but 1 to 2 months at 13 °C with high rot after three. Past about 120 days, colour is a late signal; firmness and weight loss track better.

Sample and workforce. USDA lemon inspection requires at least 25 fruit per sample. Ten fruit find a 5 % decay lot about 40 % of the time; 25 fruit about 72 %. 57 % of crop workers are Spanish-primary and 27 % speak no English, but 96 % carry a phone with internet.

The hold budget the model has to respect

Dark green5 to 6 months at 10 °C
Light greenabout 2 months
Silverabout 6 weeks
Yellowweeks; sell trigger at 5 % decay

Trade ladder from NSW DPI and the August research. The rate between rungs is set by room temperature, not by a constant, which is why the linear forecast with a fixed buffer needs a temperature band per room and a separate decay clock for weeks spent at or above 13 °C.

  • Limoneira's Q4 FY2025 release carried internally inconsistent full-year figures. The numbers used are the ones that reconcile with the 10-K.
  • Vendor pages establish advertised capabilities, not verified performance.
  • Local demo accounts were given a temporary password for the walk-through; nothing else in the database was changed.
