# Sampling station hardware, one per plant

## Board

- `board.svg` is the print file: 24 x 18 inches, generated by `python hardware/make_board.py` from the layout in `sampling/board.py`. Regenerate after any layout change; never edit the SVG by hand.
- Substrate: rigid matte black. Black aluminium composite panel (Dibond-style) or black PVC foam board 3 to 5 mm. Avoid gloss.
- Markers and patches: print the SVG at 100 percent on a wide-format printer, or print only the four marker pads and the two patch strips on matte photo paper, laminate with **matte** film, and mount them on the black board at the positions in the SVG. Glossy lamination reflects the light and ruins the patch readings.
- Each ArUco marker (DICT_4X4_50, ids 0 to 3) is 2 inches with a 0.3 inch white pad around it. The white pad is required: the detector cannot see a black marker border on a black board.
- The nine patch colors in the file are nominal sRGB targets. After printing, measure each patch with a colorimeter or spectrophotometer under a D65-compatible illuminant and update the reference values. Do not calibrate a phone image using reference values read from that same uncalibrated phone image.

## Light

- One fixed LED panel, 5000 K, CRI 90 or better, about 30 to 40 inches above the board, diffused. No mixed light: block windows and switch off nearby sodium or warm fixtures if they reach the board.
- The scoring pipeline corrects color using the grey and color patches, so the light only needs to be consistent, not perfect.

## Phone and mount

- Any recent iPhone or Android phone. Camera in default photo mode, no filters, flash off, HDR off if the option exists.
- A fixed mount (clamp arm or overhead bracket) about 30 inches above the board centre so the whole board fills the frame with a little margin. Framing does not need to be exact: the four markers rectify the image.
- Add the site to the home screen (Share, then Add to Home Screen) so it opens like an app; login lasts 30 days.

## Sampling procedure (under 90 seconds)

1. Pull 10 fruit without choosing by appearance: two fruit from each of at least five bins or containers, spread across accessible front/middle/back positions. Lay them in two rows inside the dashed zone, none touching and none on the patches.
2. Open Lemon Tracker, tap the lot, tap Take photo, shoot, three taps (color, pack within, decay count), Save.
3. Put the fruit back. Scoring happens in the background; if the board shows "retake" next visit, space the fruit out and shoot again.

## Network

Photos upload immediately; there is no offline queue in v1. Confirm wifi or cellular coverage at the station before the first field week.
